You label New York City community board budget requests for a study of where public electric-vehicle (EV) chargers are needed.

Each request has two parts. The Request line is a category from a fixed city list and is often generic. The Explanation is the board's own words. Base the label mainly on the Explanation and use the Request line for context. Label from the text alone.

Choose exactly one label.

## Labels

ev_charging: The board asks for charging that drivers of electric road vehicles can use: cars, vans, trucks, taxis or for-hire vehicles. Road vehicles only: ferries, boats, e-bikes and scooters don't count. Includes new chargers, more chargers, repairs, or a study or siting plan. Counts even when it is one sentence inside a request about something else.

parking_or_curb: The main ask is about parking or how curb space is used: garages, municipal lots, meters, permits, loading zones, or parking lost to other uses.

clean_air: The main ask is cutting air pollution or vehicle emissions: idling enforcement, air monitors, truck emissions, electric buses, or electrifying a city agency's own fleet, including ferries.

other: Everything else, including "charge" in other senses and charging for e-bikes, scooters, phones or batteries.

Label what is requested, not why. A request for chargers that gives a clean-air reason is still ev_charging.

## Rulings for hard cases

1. Chargers only for a city agency's fleet or a bus depot: clean_air.
2. E-bike, scooter, battery or phone charging: other.
3. EV chargers as a secondary ask inside another request: ev_charging.
4. Commercial drivers, or vehicle type unclear: ev_charging, and state the doubt in the reason.
5. Asks to prioritize, study, site or repair chargers: ev_charging.
6. Mentions EVs but asks for something else, or opposes chargers that take parking: label by the actual ask.
7. Street trees justified by air quality: other.
8. Request fits several labels: choose the first that applies in this order: ev_charging, parking_or_curb, clean_air, other.
9. Lane changes (road diets, lane removals, new bus or bike lanes): other, unless the request is about parking or curb space; then parking_or_curb.

## Parking flag

parking_concern is "yes" when the board raises parking or curb space as a need or a problem: parking that residents or visitors use (curb spaces, public lots or garages) or another curb use such as loading zones, whatever its label. Asking for chargers at the curb or in a parking lot doesn't count by itself. It is "no" for fleet depots, private truck lots and idling.

## Output

reason: one sentence naming what the board asks for and the definition or ruling that decided the label.
label: ev_charging, parking_or_curb, clean_air or other.
parking_concern: yes or no.
